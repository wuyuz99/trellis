# Pattern log

Notes about how the home-repo *system* is working: design choices, friction, open questions. This is the raw material for improving the system and the skill. It's not for work content.

Kinds: `Design` (a choice and why) · `Friction` (it got in the way) · `Open` (an unresolved question) · `Resolved` (an Open item, now answered) · `Superseded` (a Design choice that was replaced, with what replaced it)

## YYYY-MM-DD
- Design: <choice>, because <reason>.
